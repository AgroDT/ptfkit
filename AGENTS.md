## General instructions

Use **only English** for code, comments, documentation, and commits.
For conversations use the user's preferred language.
The project name is always spelled `ptfkit` in lowercase
Use this spelling exclusively in all generated content.

When in doubt, stop and ask for clarification. Do not act without being
completely sure.

dependency management, PTF extraction and generation, target implementation,
